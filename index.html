Mengjia Chen

PhD Candidate in Economics, University of Geneva

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Geneva. My research is in applied labor economics and economic history, with a focus on gender, family and mental health.

My current projects include a randomized controlled trial on mental health, a study of gender differences in time use and well-being, work on religion, gender norms, migration and human capital, and a project on divorce in China that examines gender differences in court rulings, including the role of judges.

Before joining the PhD program, I received an MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and a BSc in International Economics and Trade from the University of Nottingham Ningbo China.
